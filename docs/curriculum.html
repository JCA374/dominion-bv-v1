Every run's stages: the world, the reward, the check that ends the stage, the cap and the expected outcome, written down before the stage starts.

What earlier projects taught. RL-v3's turn planner, which scored action sequences by a value net after one turn, gave no gain in full games. AZ-v1's buy search with GA continuations reached 62.5% against the GA base, far below RL-v3. Here the teacher plays to the true end with RL-v3 itself, and a gate checks the teacher before any distillation. The first gate (margin 0.04, 16–32 draws) lost to c2 at 41.5% after 100 games: overriding on noisy estimates (winner's curse). The student averages noise across many positions, and every round's dev match is the real test.
