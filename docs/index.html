Learn what each buy is worth, then beat the best Dominion model

RL-v3 (c1 2300 and c2 2654) is our strongest Dominion model: 86% against the GA base. This project asks whether a small network that scores every possible buy can beat it. The network learns from paired rollouts that play each buy to the end of the game. Goal: at least 53% against both RL-v3 checkpoints on 2,000 reserved kingdoms with both seats, with the kingdom-block 95% interval above 50%.

What this is

In Dominion, most of the game is decided by what you buy. Every turn you have some coins and choose a card from the supply, or nothing. RL-v3 plays every decision with one network trained by PPO. Its buy choice is a policy: it says what it would buy, not how much better that buy is than the next best.

Example. You have $5 on turn 6 in a kingdom with Witch, Laboratory, Market and Militia. c2 buys Laboratory. The teacher plays the game to the end six ways: buy Laboratory, Market, Witch, Silver, Duchy, or nothing. Each way uses the same 16–32 shuffles of the unseen cards, and c2 plays everything afterwards. If Witch wins 61% of those games and Laboratory 55%, the label says Witch is worth six points more here.

Why it is hard. Buys differ by a few percent of win chance, while one rollout is a coin flip. The teacher's per-position estimate is noisy, and the student has to average that noise away across many positions.

Approach

Four parts:

  1. Teacher (C, c/bv.c). At each buy of the c2 seat, the candidates are c2's top 5 buys, its own choice and PASS. Each candidate is played to the end on the same hidden-state draws, a paired comparison. The unseen cards are put in canonical order and reshuffled, so the teacher never uses what the player could not see. Continuation: c2 for us, the real opponent for them.
  2. Labels: about 2–3 positions per second per core, from games against a fixed mix of c2, c1, rl-v1 2200 and the GA portfolio-v3 base. Kingdoms are uniform, plus attack, Gardens, trasher and engine slices.
  3. Student: a Deep Sets network over the 33 card tokens, 50k parameters. It scores all legal buys and PASS in one pass, and overrides c2 only by more than a margin chosen on dev kingdoms.
  4. Curriculum: labels → student fit → DAgger (the student plays, the teacher grades) → promotion match.
